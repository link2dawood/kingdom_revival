Questions

Partnership FAQ

Can I change or stop my partnership?

Yes. You can change your amount, switch to a different ministry or stop at any time — just contact us and we will take care of it.

How is my monthly gift set up?

After you sign up, use the “Set up your monthly gift” button to complete your gift through our secure giving page. Choose the monthly option there.

Do I have to give money to be a partner?

Not at all. Prayer Partners are just as important to us. Prayer is the foundation of everything we do.

Can my church or small group partner together?

Yes. Choose “Church / Group Partner” and tell us about your church or group. We would love to serve alongside you.

How will I hear from you?

We will keep in touch by email with stories, prayer points and news from the field. You can unsubscribe any time.
