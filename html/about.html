About Us

A Gospel-centred ministry serving Pakistan’s forgotten

We preach Jesus Christ, plant churches, equip leaders and care for orphans, widows and brick kiln families — so that the forgotten know they are loved by God.

Partner with us Meet our founder

Who we are

Bringing the hope of Jesus to those the world overlooks

Kingdom Revival Ministries is based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. We serve unreached villages, marginalised communities and families who live and work beside the brick kilns — many of them trapped in poverty and debt bondage.

Our mission is to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ, proclaiming the Gospel among unreached communities, planting churches, equipping leaders, and serving vulnerable children and brick kiln families.

Our mission

Make disciples. Serve the vulnerable.

To glorify God by proclaiming the Gospel, planting churches, equipping leaders and showing Christ’s love through practical care.

Our vision

Revival that reaches every village

Communities across Pakistan transformed by the Gospel — with thriving local churches, freed families and children who grow up knowing Jesus.

What we do

Word and deed, hand in hand

Everything we do flows from the Gospel and is expressed through practical love.

Proclaim the Gospel

Taking the good news of Jesus Christ to villages and unreached communities across Pakistan.

Plant churches

Planting and strengthening local and house churches where believers can gather and grow.

Equip leaders

Discipling believers and training pastors and leaders to shepherd their communities.

Care for the vulnerable

Serving orphans, widows and brick kiln families with education, aid and love.
