Questions

Giving FAQ

Which payment method should I use?

PayPal is the quickest way to give from anywhere in the world. Bank transfer works well for larger or regular gifts. Western Union and MoneyGram are helpful if you prefer to send cash transfers.

How do I give monthly?

Set up a standing order or recurring transfer with your bank using the account details above, or send your gift through PayPal each month. Let us know so we can thank you and keep you updated.

How do I tell you what my gift is for?

Choose the ministry in the gift form, then use “Let us know about your gift” to email us. Adding the ministry name in your bank or PayPal reference also helps.

Who can I contact with questions?

Call or WhatsApp us on +92-341-5418733, or email Info@kingdomrevivalministries.com. We are happy to help.
